# FOMOD Plus

FOMOD Plus installs configurable mods and identifies optional additions that fit the user's mod setup.

## Language

**Patch suggestion**:
A FOMOD option that qualifies as a potential addition for the current mod setup. A qualifying option can be dismissed from the suggestions list without losing its eligibility when browsing.
_Avoid_: Available patch (as a synonym for a suggestion)

**Dismissal**:
A user's choice to hide an option from the suggestions list without removing it from browsing or changing its eligibility.
_Avoid_: Deselection (a choice not to install an option, rather than a choice to hide a suggestion)

**Stale suggestions**:
Previously established patch suggestions whose current eligibility could not be verified because a refresh failed. They describe the last successful refresh, not necessarily the current mod setup.
_Avoid_: Current suggestions, no suggestions (when describing a failed refresh)
